Privacy Policy

What we collect

If you join the Zikaroo waitlist, we collect the information you choose to submit in the waitlist form. That currently includes:

  • Your email address
  • Your first name if you choose to provide it
  • The submission timestamp and source tracking note for the launch site

Why we collect it

We use this information to:

  • Manage early-access interest in Zikaroo
  • Send launch updates or invite selected users to try the product
  • Respond to support or partnership inquiries

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist and support data for as long as it is reasonably needed to manage the launch process, communicate with interested users, and maintain basic business records.

Your choices

You can request that we remove your waitlist information or stop contacting you by emailing support@zikaroo.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or data removal requests, contact support@zikaroo.com.
